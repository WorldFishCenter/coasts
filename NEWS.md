# coasts 0.5.0

## A new look and a clearer Country insights page

* **NEW** New look across the site, built on the Peskas brand colours, with a dark theme by default and a light one.
* **NEW** The map opens on the whole coast from Kenya to Mozambique, with Timor-Leste one click away, and clicking a district opens a sheet with its main figures.
* **NEW** Country insights rebuilt: each country's trend and all its districts or provinces ranked in one table.
* **FIXED** Districts without survey data no longer look like districts with low values.

# coasts 0.4.1

## Clearer fishing activity maps

* **FIXED** Areas visited by fewer than three trips are no longer shown, so the map highlights reliable fishing activity only.
* **FIXED** Column heights for average fishing hours per active day now display correctly after a data correction.
* **NEW** Clearer names, units and definitions for fishing intensity and constancy across the map, tooltips and glossary.
* **FIXED** Tooltips no longer repeat the selected metric.

# coasts 0.4.0

## In-app guide to the data

* **NEW** A documentation page explains data sources, metrics, map layers, methods and limitations, with links from the map.
* **NEW** A help button on the map explains what each layer shows.
* **NEW** The legend explains colour scales and shows how recent the data is.
* **FIXED** Metric names, units and descriptions are now consistent across the map, sidebar, country view and documentation.
